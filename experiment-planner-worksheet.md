## Want More Structure?

This template covers the essentials for most sandbox experiments. If your experiment needs deeper tracking, consider extending with:

For Quantitative/Technical Work:
- Data validation protocols, reproducibility logs, safety compliance

For Research & Writing:
- Source attribution tracking, bias detection, research ethics documentation

For Creative & Design Work:
- Copyright/licensing tracking, design rationale documentation

For Multilingual Content:
- Translation verification, cross-language output comparison

For Assessment Development:
- Rubric calibration notes, student feedback integration

For Accessibility:
- WCAG compliance checklists, multi-modal delivery tracking

Need inspiration? Check the shared exemplars in the Examples library: /user/examples/shared
