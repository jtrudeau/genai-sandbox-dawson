GenAI Sandbox

The GenAI Sandbox at Dawson College supports practical experimentation with generative AI in teaching, learning, and academic support work.

The idea is simple: choose one task, try AI with enough context to judge the result, review what happened, and decide what to do next. Experiments stay small, transparent, and easy to pause. Educator judgment stays central throughout.

This site is for educators who want a clear, responsible process for testing possibilities in their own context. No prior generative AI experience is required.
