Use this menu to choose **small, safe experiments** that fit your course or workshop.

## Assessment & Feedback
1. **MCQ Generation & Review (Mechanics):** AI + SME workflow for crafting concept/quantitative questions with fair distractors, including QA checklist and analytics loop. See the Physics MCQ example in the [Faculty Examples](/examples/faculty/physics-mcq).
2. **Code Review Assist (Arduino Robotics Lab):** Claude/ChatGPT aids student revisions on Arduino sketches in a physics/engineering robotics context, prototyping a low-stakes feedback pipeline. See the Arduino Robotics Code Review example in [Faculty Examples](/user/examples/faculty/arduino-robotics).
3. **Essay Feedback Calibration (Humanities):** AI provides rubric-aligned high-level feedback; faculty refine tone and depth for cohort consistency.

## Creative Assignment Redesign
4. **Word-Weaving with AI as Reflective Partner (Anthropology):** In-class creative work (phrase/metaphor invention) + AI conversation for refinement and reflection. Protects authentic student creativity while using AI meaningfully. See the [Anthropology Word-Weaving example](/user/examples/faculty/anthropology).

## Content & Workflow Support
5. **Industrial Design Co-Designer (LLM + Lab Constraints):** LLM-generated briefs and constraint checklists vetted by students/instructor to accelerate prototyping. See the Industrial Design example in [Faculty Examples](/examples/faculty/industrial-design).
6. **Applied Arts Moodboard + Rationale (Archived):** Midjourney/Firefly outputs annotated for brand alignment (available in the [Examples Archive](/examples/archive)).

## Lab & Practical Prep (Archived / Optional)
7. **Physics Lab Pre-Brief (Archived):** AI-generated pre-lab guidance with safety review; useful reference if lab teams revisit the idea (see the [Examples Archive](/examples/archive)).

## Emerging Ideas to Explore
8. **Rubric Calibration & Peer Review:** AI suggests exemplar responses, faculty adjust for clarity, then use in peer feedback training.
9. **Reflection Prompt Tailoring:** AI drafts post-activity reflections based on tracked misconceptions; faculty decide where to deploy.
10. **Resource Menu Builder:** AI curates topic-specific practice sets or reading lists, faculty validate and contextualize.
11. **Accessibility Audit Assist:** AI identifies accessibility gaps (alt text, contrast) in digital teaching artifacts for quick faculty review.
