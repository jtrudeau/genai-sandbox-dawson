GENAI Sandbox – Faculty Exploration Template (Applied Arts Simulation)

Sandbox Overview

“This sandbox concept is your space to explore AI-enhanced teaching before classroom deployment. Share what you learn, keep materials CC-BY where possible, and flag accessibility or integrity questions during the monthly pulse. The checklist below keeps governance light; use it as a reminder, not a barrier.”

  • Transparency: I can explain any AI-generated material included here.
  • Student data & privacy: Team boards reviewed for personal info; NDA guidelines respected.
  • Licensing: Midjourney/Firefly usage documented; partner approval in progress.
  • Notes / Exceptions: Partner brand requires final sign-off before public showcase.

1. Exploration Goal Snapshot

  • Goal (1–2 sentences): Help design students use AI moodboards and layout suggestions to accelerate packaging iterations while articulating human design decisions.
  • Intended audience: 24 second-year graphic design students (teams of 3).
  • Why this matters now: Studio critiques stall when AI-generated visuals lack rationale, slowing iteration cycles.
  • Special requirements or constraints (accessibility, departmental needs, etc.): Partner brand mandates NDA compliance; course rubric requires originality and production feasibility documentation.

2. Workflow & Experiment Log

StepAI AssistHuman JudgmentEvidence / Notes
1Midjourney generates initial moodboard based on brand keywordsInstructor and student team curate selections, discard off-brand conceptsPrompt and outputs captured in FigJam; off-brand examples tagged “discard”
2Adobe Firefly produces packaging layout variationsStudents adjust typography, color, hierarchy to align with brand standardsLayout iterations saved with annotation layer noting human edits
3Teams annotate AI-assisted boards in FigJam, adding rationale for each choiceInstructor reviews annotations, prompts deeper critique questionsAnnotated FigJam export with audio critique snippets attached
4Studio critique session: peers evaluate concepts referencing annotated boardsInstructor moderates critique, ensures notes tie back to rubricCritique summary recorded; rubric scores logged
5Teams finalize concept and document AI vs. human contributionsInstructor confirms originality and production feasibilityFinal concept storyboard plus “AI contribution log” uploaded
6Post-sprint reflection: students identify what they’ll keep/change for next sprintInstructor synthesizes common themes for next moduleReflection notes stored in course LMS
  • Iteration notes (adjustments, surprises, prompts worth saving): Midjourney prompt required specifying packaging format to avoid irrelevant outputs; Firefly color palettes needed manual adjustment for brand contrast guidelines.

3. Outputs & Artifacts

  • Artifact 1 (link / location): FigJam board export with AI prompt logs and annotated critique notes (examples/appliedarts-sim-board.pdf).
  • Artifact 2 (optional): AI contribution log template completed by Team B.
  • Supporting media or walkthrough: Audio highlight reel (5 min) summarizing critique insights.
  • Audience feedback captured (if any): Partner brand noted improved rationale clarity; students requested quick reference for acceptable AI edit documentation.

4. Reflection Check-In

  1. What shifted during the sandbox run?
    • Teams delivered two rounds of viable concepts within 3 weeks (previously 4–5 weeks); critiques referenced brand language more consistently.
  2. How did AI help or hinder your approach?
    • Helped by rapidly exploring visual directions; hindered when AI outputs mimicked competitor branding—required manual filtering.
  3. What will you keep, change, or try next?
    • Keep annotated FigJam critique workflow; change prompt templates to include brand do/don’ts; next, pilot AI-assisted accessibility checks.

5. Verification Snapshot

Output / DecisionHow I verified itConfidence (low/med/high)
Midjourney moodboardsReviewed against brand guideline checklist; removed non-compliant imageryMedium
Firefly layoutsCompared typography hierarchy with rubric; ran color contrast check manuallyHigh
AI contribution logCross-referenced annotations with final concept file to confirm human editsHigh
  • Follow-up questions for coordination (policy, feasibility, other constraints): Need guidance on storing AI outputs under NDA; confirm if we can access institution-shared Midjourney account for licensing clarity.

6. Next Experiment Hooks

  • Next idea I want to explore: Integrate AI accessibility audit tools into layout workflow.
  • Support I could use (community feedback, resources, tooling): Examples of AI contribution logs from other departments; contact in legal to confirm NDA scope.
  • Planned artifact (department meeting, sandbox cohort session, etc.): Showcase at Applied Arts showcase and sandbox orientation.

Optional Persona Prompt Sidebar

  • Starter prompts: “What if AI handled first-wave moodboards while we annotate rationale?”
  • Optimizer prompts: “How can we document AI edits so the critique stays focused on human decisions?”
