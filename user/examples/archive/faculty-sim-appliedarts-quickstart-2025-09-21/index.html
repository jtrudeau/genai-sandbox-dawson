Faculty Simulation – Applied Arts Persona Quickstart

Persona Snapshot

  • Department: Applied Arts (Graphic Design studio)
  • Instructor Goal: Help second-year design students iterate packaging concepts using AI while maintaining original authorship and brand guidelines.
  • Course Context: 12-week studio course; students balance digital prototyping with physical mockups.

1. Set Exploration Goal

  • Challenge: Students lean on AI-generated visuals without documenting decision rationale, causing critiques to stall.
  • Prototype Goal: Pilot a workflow where AI moodboards and layout suggestions feed into student-led critique boards with design rationale annotations.
  • Audience: 24 second-year graphic design students working in teams of 3.

2. Map the Sandbox Run

  • Tools to Trial: Midjourney (moodboards), Adobe Firefly (layout variations), FigJam board for critique annotations.
  • Workflow Snapshot Draft: Step through moodboard prompts → layout iterations → team critique annotations → final selection notes.
  • Rapid Test: Apply to one product packaging sprint (3 weeks) with brand guidelines from industry partner.

3. Experiment & Capture Learnings

  • Log prompts and AI outputs in shared FigJam space; capture revisions and rationale via sticky notes.
  • Track critique quality using rubric focusing on concept originality, brand alignment, and production feasibility.
  • Risks: Students over-rely on AI aesthetics; license compliance for Midjourney outputs.

4. Prep Share-Out Artifact

  • Deliverable: FigJam board export showing AI prompt logs, student annotations, and final concept storyboard.
  • Story: Goal → AI-assisted exploration → human critique decisions → next sprint adjustments.
  • Evidence: Before/after critique snapshots, audio transcript highlights from critique session.

5. Reflection & Next Steps

  • Questions: Did AI-assisted iterations speed up concept exploration? Were critiques richer (more brand vocabulary)?
  • Next Experiment Idea: Introduce AI-generated accessibility checks (color contrast, typography) for packaging designs.

6. Safety Guardrails

  • Integrity: Require students to tag AI-generated assets and document edits.
  • Student data & privacy: Review team FigJam boards for personal data; keep partner brand assets within NDA guidelines.
  • Licensing: Confirm Midjourney/Firefly commercial use terms with partner; attribute where required.

Additional Notes

  • Special requirement: Partner brand requires sign-off on any AI-generated imagery before external sharing.
  • Coordination ask: Provide template language for documenting AI edits in critique boards.
