GENAI Sandbox – Faculty Exploration Template (Industrial Design Simulation)

Last updated: 2025-09-21

Sandbox Overview

“This sandbox concept is your space to explore AI-enhanced teaching before classroom deployment. Share what you learn, keep materials CC-BY where possible, and flag integrity questions during the monthly pulse. The checklist below keeps governance light; use it as a reminder, not a barrier.”

  • Transparency: I can explain any AI-generated material included here.
  • Student data & privacy: Prompt logs scrubbed; stored in approved course workspace.
  • Licensing: GPT-5 workspace terms under review; awaiting confirmation from coordination team.
  • Notes / Exceptions: External material database referenced—citation logged in Miro board.

1. Exploration Goal Snapshot

  • Goal (1–2 sentences): Enable teams to co-create structured design briefs with an LLM, freeing more studio time for prototyping while preserving human judgment.
  • Intended audience: 20 third-year industrial design students (five teams of four).
  • Why this matters now: Teams spend 8–10 hours drafting briefs before prototyping, compressing build time and limiting iteration cycles.
  • Special requirements or constraints (accessibility, departmental needs, etc.): Accreditation requires storing design rationale; physical prototype due by week 6.

2. Workflow & Experiment Log

6Post-sprint review: teams document LLM contributions vs. human adjustmentsInstructor consolidates insights for studio retrospectiveContribution log stored in LMS; highlights on Miro
  • Iteration notes: LLM initially recommended carbon fiber despite budget constraints—prompt adjusted to emphasize low-cost materials. Rhino plugin caught dimension mismatch; team resolved before build.

3. Outputs & Artifacts

  • Artifact 1 (link / location): Miro export containing prompts, annotated outputs, and constraint checklist (examples/industrialdesign-sim-board.pdf).
  • Artifact 2 (optional): Contribution log spreadsheet summarizing accepted vs. rejected LLM suggestions.
  • Supporting media or walkthrough: Prototype photo collage with callouts linking back to refined brief elements.
  • Audience feedback captured (if any): Teams reported saving ~6 hours on brief creation; mentor noted clearer constraint documentation.

4. Reflection Check-In

  1. What shifted during the sandbox run?
    • Drafting time dropped from ~9 hours to 3 hours, allowing two additional prototyping iterations within the sprint.
  2. How did AI help or hinder your approach?
    • Helped by structuring briefs and surfacing overlooked constraints; hindered when suggesting materials beyond lab capabilities.
  3. What will you keep, change, or try next?
    • Keep LLM-assisted constraint checklists; change prompts to pre-filter by lab inventory; next, trial LLM-generated testing scenarios versus actual performance data.

5. Verification Snapshot

Output / DecisionHow I verified itConfidence (low/med/high)
GPT-5 design briefCross-checked against user research notes; instructor validated scope coverageMedium
Material suggestionsCompared with lab inventory and supplier specs; removed non-compliant optionsHigh
Constraint checklistReviewed with accreditation rubric; ensured documentation stored in LMSHigh
  • Follow-up questions for coordination (policy, feasibility, other constraints): Clarify retention period for prompt logs; confirm process for sharing GPT-5 workspace across teams and finalizing licensing review.

6. Next Experiment Hooks

  • Next idea I want to explore: Pair LLM-generated test scenarios with real prototype data to assess feasibility.
  • Support I could use (community feedback, resources, tooling): Examples from engineering department on constraint validation; guidance on centralizing prompt logs.
  • Planned share-out (department meeting, sandbox cohort session, etc.): Present during industrial design brown-bag and sandbox orientation showcase.

Optional Persona Prompt Sidebar

  • Starter prompts: “Draft a design brief for a commuter accessory used in rainy climates…”
  • Optimizer prompts: “Identify constraints a prototyping team might miss for this brief; prioritize them by risk.”
