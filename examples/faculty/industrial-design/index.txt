## Overview

**Department:** Industrial Design / Engineering  
**Course Context:** Design studios and prototyping labs  
**AI Tools:** GPT-5 for design briefs and constraint analysis  
**Goal:** Accelerate prototyping while maintaining design rigor through constraint validation

- **Constraint validation checklists** for design feasibility
- **Lab-ready prototyping** acceleration
- **Student-instructor collaboration** in design validation

## Learning Objectives

Students will:
- Learn to collaborate effectively with AI design tools
- Develop systematic constraint validation skills
- Accelerate ideation while maintaining design quality
- Practice critical evaluation of AI-generated design concepts

2. **Ideation Support:** AI suggests approaches while students maintain creative control
3. **Constraint Validation:** Systematic checking against technical and practical limits

This simulation explores how AI can serve as a co-designer in industrial design courses, helping with constraint validation and prototyping acceleration.

## Overview

**Department:** Industrial Design / Engineering  
**Course Context:** Design studios and prototyping labs  
**AI Tools:** GPT-5 for design briefs and constraint analysis  
**Goal:** Accelerate prototyping while maintaining design rigor through constraint validation

- **Constraint validation checklists** for design feasibility

- **Student-instructor collaboration** in design validation

- Learn to collaborate effectively with AI design tools
- Develop systematic constraint validation skills
