23:T5c7,# About the GenAI Sandbox

The GenAI Sandbox is a Dawson College initiative that supports practical, reflective experimentation with generative AI in teaching and learning. The sandbox was developed in collaboration with Concordia University as part of the PIM project *Be prepared to thrive in the AI era*, and PIM grant support helped develop the Workshop-in-a-Box resources shared on this site. Across workshops and ongoing faculty experimentation, the project focuses on responsible, safe, and pedagogically sound use of generative AI in higher education.

This documentation brings together facilitation materials, experiment guides, prompts, and reusable templates that educators and support staff can adapt to their own contexts. The goal is to help teams test sandboxed use cases, document evidence, and refine practice over time while building institutional capacity.

## Acknowledgements

Joel Trudeau led the development of the GenAI Sandbox project and documentation at Dawson College. We thank Robert Stephens for collaboration in developing and running iterative sandbox experiments that informed the workshop model and related resources. We also acknowledge the contributions of IRV, an AI agent persona designed as an example of sandbox practice, specifically for assisting in the technical development and maintenance of this documentation site.
