About

About the GenAI Sandbox

The GenAI Sandbox is a Dawson College initiative that supports practical, reflective experimentation with generative AI in teaching and learning. The sandbox was developed in collaboration with Concordia University as part of the inter-order PIM project Be prepared to thrive in the AI era, and PIM grant support helped develop the Workshop-in-a-Box resources shared on this site. Across workshops and ongoing faculty experimentation, the project focuses on responsible, safe, and pedagogically sound use of generative AI in higher education.

This documentation brings together facilitation materials, experiment guides, prompts, and reusable templates that educators and support staff can adapt to their own contexts. The goal is to help teams test bounded use cases, document evidence, and refine practice over time while building institutional capacity.
