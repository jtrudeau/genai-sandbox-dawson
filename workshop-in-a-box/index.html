📦 Workshop-in-a-BoxWorkshop Overview

Workshop-in-a-Box

Use this section to run a standalone faculty workshop for exploring generative AI in teaching and learning.

The workshop is designed as a structured starting point, not as a full professional-development program. Participants leave with one or more sandboxed experiment ideas and a path to continue.

A sandbox format supports responsible use: keep scope limited, keep educator judgment central, and document evidence before deciding what to scale.

Workshop Shape

The model run uses three parts:

  • framing and live demonstration
  • guided hands-on exploration
  • synthesis, next steps, and continuity resources

This structure can be adapted for 60, 90, or 120 minutes while preserving the same core logic.

Participants should move from ideas to one concrete run.

Department Adaptation

For department-specific workshops, narrow the task, pre-configure the AI context, and keep peer discussion around shared artifacts.

Intended Outcomes

  • clearer faculty judgment about when AI support is useful
  • tested, documented examples of sandboxed practice
  • stronger cross-colleague conversation about pedagogical fit, risk, and value

The goal is practical capacity building through experimentation and reflection.
