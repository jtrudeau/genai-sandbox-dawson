24:T782,# Streamlined Template Spine – Shared Elements (Draft)

_Last updated: 2025-09-21_

## Shared Governance Preface (≤120 words)
- Welcome to the GENAI Sandbox: focus on exploration, iteration, and storytelling.
- Reminder of CC-BY sharing, transparency, and accessibility commitments.
- Mention that integrity checks live inside the template; governance team available via monthly pulse if questions arise.

## Section Order & Required Fields
1. **Exploration Goal Snapshot**
   - Goal statement (1–2 sentences)
   - Audience focus (students, colleagues, self)
   - Prototype window dates
2. **Workflow & Experiment Log**
   - Table with columns: Step | AI Assist | Human Judgment | Evidence/Notes
   - Space for up to 6 steps; encourage iteration notes beneath table
3. **Outputs & Artifacts**
   - Bullet slots for artifacts generated (link or brief description)
   - Optional media reference (slide deck, video, community post)
4. **Reflection Check-In**
   - Q1: What shifted during this sandbox run?
   - Q2: How did AI help or hinder your approach?
   - Q3: What will you keep, change, or try next?
5. **Safety Guardrails & Verification**
   - Checklist (integrity, transparency, accessibility) with yes/no + notes
   - Mini-table for verification evidence (Output | How verified | Confidence)
6. **Next Experiment Hooks**
   - Planned follow-up exploration (1–2 bullets)
   - Support needed from community or coordination team

## Optional Modules (Add-on)
- Persona-specific prompt sidebar (faculty vs. student self-directed)
- Department terminology swaps (fields to rename)
- Case-study capsule callout (for coordination team use)

## Cross-Template Guidelines
- Keep instructions inline and concise; link to quickstart for full context.
- Use examples in the [Examples library](/examples/shared) for workflow table and verification entries.
