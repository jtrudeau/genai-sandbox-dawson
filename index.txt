# About the Generative AI Sandbox

The GenAI Sandbox at Dawson College supports practical, low-risk experimentation with generative AI in teaching and learning.

The goal is straightforward: run small, sandboxed experiments, document what you observe, and make informed decisions about what to continue, revise, or stop.

Using AI is not a mandate. This site is for educators who want a clear, responsible process for testing possibilities in their own context.

<Card className="border-l-4 border-l-blue-500 p-6 my-8">
  <div className="mb-2">
    No prior generative AI experience is required.
  </div>
  <div className="text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-400">
    What matters is keeping experiments sandboxed, documenting judgments, and reflecting on evidence.
  </div>
</Card>

## Why a sandbox?
<Card className="border-l-4 border-l-slate-400 p-6 my-8">
  <div className="mb-3">
    A sandbox is a responsible way to work with generative AI in education: start small, protect students and staff from unnecessary risk, and evaluate impact before scaling.
  </div>
  <ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-700 dark:text-gray-300">
    <li>Define one clear target: a learning activity, assessment task, feedback process, or workflow.</li>
    <li>Keep scope limited so the experiment is transparent, manageable, and easy to pause.</li>
    <li>Keep educator judgment central; AI supports decisions, but does not replace them.</li>
    <li>Document what happened, including benefits, limits, and integrity concerns.</li>
    <li>Use evidence to decide what to continue, revise, or stop.</li>
  </ul>
</Card>
