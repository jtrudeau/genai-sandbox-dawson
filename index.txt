24:T7af,# Welcome to the GenAI Sandbox

The sandbox is Dawson’s hub for faculty to prototype AI-assisted teaching and learning—safely, quickly, and together. Road-test assessment workflows, streamline feedback loops, and surface classroom-ready exemplars before taking them live.

## Why It Matters
- **Teach with confidence:** Explore AI practices aligned with Quebec’s Humanism, Responsibility, and Transparency principles.
- **Shareable wins:** Turn experiments into reusable artifacts ready for classrooms, workshops, or faculty showcases.
- **Community-first:** Build alongside colleagues and learning strategists, with coordinated guardrails and monthly pulse support.

## How the Sandbox Works
1. **Pick your outcome:** Start with the [Sandbox Quickstart](/quickstart) to capture an exploration goal and risks.
2. **Prototype & document:** Use the [Faculty Template](/guides/faculty-template) to log prompts, human judgment, and guardrails.
3. **Show what you learned:** Publish artifacts, contribute to workshops, and feed insights back into Dawson’s playbook.

## What You’ll Find Here
- **Explore & Launch:** [Sandbox Quickstart](/quickstart) and streamlined templates to get moving in under an hour.
- **Internal Library:** Discipline-specific simulations and workshop plans live under [Internal → Examples](/internal/examples) while we iterate.

## Get Support & Stay Aligned
- Join upcoming sessions in [Internal → Workshops](/internal/workshops) for hands-on drafting and feedback.
- Coordinate rollout and communications through the [Internal → Communications hub](/internal/communications/sandbox-docs-rollout).
- Keep safety checkpoints light but explicit using the guardrail checklists inside each template.

Looking for co-curricular experiments? Explore [Internal → SPACE Projects](/internal/space) curated by the SPACE team.
