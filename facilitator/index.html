Your Role as Facilitator

As a sandbox facilitator, you:

  • Create space for exploration and reflection
  • Guide process without prescribing solutions
  • Facilitate discussion to surface insights
  • Support experimentation with appropriate guardrails
  • Help faculty learn from both successes and failures

You are not expected to be an AI expert. Your expertise is in:

  • Creating safe environments for learning
  • Facilitating productive discussions
  • Helping people reflect on their practice
  • Managing group dynamics
