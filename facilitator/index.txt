- Handling common challenges

- How to help faculty frame experiments
- Providing feedback and guidance
- Creating psychological safety

- Cohort structures that work
- Progress tracking approaches
- Keeping momentum between sessions
- Capturing collective insights

## Your Role as Facilitator

As a sandbox facilitator, you:
- **Create space** for exploration and reflection
- **Guide process** without prescribing solutions
- **Facilitate discussion** to surface insights
- **Support experimentation** with appropriate guardrails
- **Help faculty learn** from both successes and failures

You are **not** expected to be an AI expert. Your expertise is in:
- Creating safe environments for learning
- Facilitating productive discussions
- Helping people reflect on their practice
- Managing group dynamics

## Session Types

### Launch Sessions
- Introduce the sandbox philosophy
- Help participants plan first experiments
- Set expectations and commitments

### Progress Check-ins
- Share experiences and learnings
- Troubleshoot challenges
- Refine ongoing experiments

### Focused Workshops
- Deep dive on specific topics
- Skill-building sessions
- Guest speakers or demos

### Reflection & Synthesis
- Harvest collective insights
- Celebrate experiments (successful or not)
- Plan next steps

- **[Orientation Guide](/orientation)** - Your main facilitation resource
- **Session Templates** - Ready-to-use structures (coming soon)

Welcome! This section is designed for people running sandbox workshops, supporting cohorts, and helping faculty experiment with AI in their teaching.

## What you'll find here

### 🎓 Facilitator Orientation
- [Core facilitation guide](/orientation) - How to run sandbox sessions
- Session structures and timing
- Discussion facilitation techniques
- Handling common challenges

### 👥 Supporting Faculty
- How to help faculty frame experiments
- Providing feedback and guidance
- Creating psychological safety
- Balancing structure with autonomy

### 📊 Managing Cohorts
- Cohort structures that work
- Progress tracking approaches
- Keeping momentum between sessions
- Capturing collective insights

## Your Role as Facilitator

As a sandbox facilitator, you:
- **Create space** for exploration and reflection
- **Guide process** without prescribing solutions
- **Facilitate discussion** to surface insights
- **Support experimentation** with appropriate guardrails
- **Help faculty learn** from both successes and failures

You are **not** expected to be an AI expert. Your expertise is in:
- Creating safe environments for learning
- Facilitating productive discussions
- Helping people reflect on their practice
- Managing group dynamics

## Session Types

### Launch Sessions
- Introduce the sandbox philosophy
- Help participants plan first experiments
- Set expectations and commitments

### Progress Check-ins
- Share experiences and learnings
- Troubleshoot challenges
- Refine ongoing experiments

### Focused Workshops
- Deep dive on specific topics
- Skill-building sessions
- Guest speakers or demos

### Reflection & Synthesis
- Harvest collective insights
- Celebrate experiments (successful or not)
- Plan next steps

- **[Orientation Guide](/orientation)** - Your main facilitation resource
- **Session Templates** - Ready-to-use structures (coming soon)
