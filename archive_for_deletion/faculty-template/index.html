Sandbox Overview & Guardrails”This sandbox is a space for creative exploration, guided by the Quebec Ministry principles of

Humanism, Responsibility, and Transparency. Use the guardrails below to pause on the why behind each action and keep governance light without losing intention.”

  • Transparency & Traceability – I have documented the workflow so I can explain every AI contribution.
  • Responsibility & Data Privacy – I protected personal information and respected Dawson data policies (Loi 25 aligned).
  • Equity & Integrity – I checked for bias or misleading outputs and attributed all sources/licensing exceptions.
