## Persona Snapshot
- Department: Humanities (History of Ideas seminar)
- Instructor Goal: Help students interrogate AI-generated primary-source summaries while preserving critical analysis skills.
- Course Context: Liberal Arts module exploring enlightenment texts (360-DWP-xx); limited class time for close reading.

## 
1. Set Exploration Goal- Challenge: Students rely on AI summaries instead of engaging with original texts.
- Prototype Goal: Test a workflow where AI drafts comparative summaries and students annotate discrepancies during seminar prep.
- Audience: Cohort of 32 students in the Liberal Arts program (History of Ideas seminar).
- Potential Risks & Ethical Considerations: AI summaries could flatten nuanced arguments; mitigate by pairing AI outputs with primary passages and student-led critique prompts.

## 
2. Map the Sandbox Run- Tools to Trial: ChatGPT & Claude (comparative summaries), Perplexity (source trails), Hypothes.is for annotations.
- Workflow Snapshot Draft: Present in template (steps: select text, AI compare, student annotation, reflection).
- Rapid Test: Use two upcoming readings (Kant + Wollstonecraft) over a two-week mini-cycle.

## 
3. Experiment & Capture Learnings- Plan to log prompts and AI outputs in shared drive; track student annotation quality pre/post.
- Initial expectation: AI summaries surface structural themes; students identify nuance gaps.
- Risks: Over-reliance on AI phrasing; citation accuracy.

## 
4. Prep Share-Out Artifact- Deliverable: Slide deck + annotated sample showing student annotations vs. AI summary.
- Story: Goal → workflow → findings on student engagement → next tweak.
- Evidence: Screenshots of Hypothes.is threads, before/after reflection snippets.

## 
5. Reflection & Next Steps- Questions: Did annotated workflow increase primary text references in discussion? What adjustments improve prompt specificity?
- Next Experiment Idea: Integrate AI-generated discussion starters validated by student teams.

## 
6. Safety GuardrailsGuided by the Ministry principles of **Humanism**, **Responsibility**, and **Transparency**:
- [ ] **Transparency & Traceability**: Document prompts and cite any AI-generated summaries so students can compare sources.
- [ ] **Responsibility & Data Privacy**: Archive annotations in FERPA-compliant folders, anonymize screenshots, and rely on institution-backed accounts (Loi 25 aligned).
- [ ] **Equity & Integrity**: Cross-check citations against original texts, surface diverse viewpoints, and attribute AI contributions in artifacts.

- Special requirement: Departmental mandate to archive Hypothes.is threads each term and log accessibility compliance.
